The problems

swap: day and month swap places

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a date whose day is 12 or lower comes out with day and month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July” (R-41200). This is the fix #4471 has queued. It covers every tenant and every channel.

The table on #4471 splits these into parser and hint. The hint rule takes a report that came in through the support form at a booking-v3 tenant as a booking made through the booking-v3 form. A report’s channel is how it reached the desk, not how the booking was made, and the reports in hint read exactly like the rest: the same swap, the same tenants’ complaints. Nothing in them points at a second bug, so they stay in this one ticket. If booking-v3’s MM/DD/YYYY hint turns out to cause swaps of its own, the parser fix will show it: those reports will keep coming.

one-day: the date moves by one day

These reports have no swap: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May” (R-41202), “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting” (R-41344), “Every booking in June is one day off” (R-41209). A day that moves by one looks like a time-zone or daylight-saving problem in how invites are sent, not a parsing one. The desk had read these apart (oneDayMove), but the #4471 table still counts them under parser, so a parser fix would have been expected to close them.

Intake tags are not the cause

Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser. The browser does not decide what went wrong; the report does. Do not triage these problems by tag.
