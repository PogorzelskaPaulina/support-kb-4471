The problems

parser: the shared parser reads month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a date whose day is 12 or lower comes out with day and month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July” (R-41200). This is the fix #4471 has queued. It covers every tenant through the app and email, and the booking-v2 tenants through the form.

Some of these reports read like a date that moved by one day (desk-notes.json). Their tenant and channel place them here, and they stay counted here, with the rest of the parser reports.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first tenants to type month first

Tenants on booking-v3 show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks like parser (a swapped day and month), but the parser is not involved: fixing it changes nothing here. The fix is the hint, and the field’s input order, following the tenant’s locale. The table on #4471 already marked this group “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”. It has one now.

Intake tags are not the cause

Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser, the moment it comes in. The cause depends on the tenant, and the two disagree: the table shows what intake tagged each problem’s reports. Do not split or count these problems by tag.

What changes

  • Each problem is tracked in its own ticket (linked in the table), with its own count. The fix queued for #4471 belongs to parser; hint needs a fix of its own in booking-v3.
  • New reports about a wrong date: look up the tenant before you merge. A report through the form at a booking-v3 tenant is hint; the rest is parser.
  • Decision 0001 accepted that reports with different subjects may end up apart. #4471 shows the other side: reports with the same subjects and different causes end up together.
